Features

No compromise precision.

You are free to scale up your planets using real-world measurements and distances without having to worry about precision loss, z-flighting, and shadow distances.

Realtime speed and performance.

Experience unparalleled speed and efficiency with real-time rendering, ensuring smooth performance during both editing and playback.

Pre-made planets.

Save time and effort with pre-made planetary systems, that can instantly transform your scene and serve as a starting point for further customisation.

Planetary rings

can be customized to build eye-catching worlds. Rings interact with the atmosphere and the planet surface - cast shadows and indirect light.

Animate everything.

Use keyframes to effortlessly animate each feature, giving you the ability to bring your scenes to life.

Intuitive Planet Positioning.

When you work in celestial sizes, intuitive positioning is a must. You can position planets using orbital, global, or viewer transform.

Cinematic Atmosphere.

Our atmosphere is built with a multi-layer system. You can assign properties to each layer to build your own unique atmospheres. Our physics based ray-marcher takes care of mixing it all together.

Save time.

PCO saves hours that usually are invested in solving issues that come up when you decide to create planetary-scale worlds.

Works on Eevee & Cycles.

Our addon seamlessly integrates with both Eevee and Cycles render engines, ensuring compatibility and consistent results across your favorite rendering platforms.

Lights up your scene.

Our addon is calculated in World node shader that automatically acts as environment light and illuminates your scene without having to add fake lights manually.

Physically acurrate shading.

Our planet surface lighting model is based on Hapke BRDF specifically designed for celestial bodies. Raytraced soft shadows are cast from planets. Indirect light bounces from lit parts of planets.

to make procedural worlds you won't be able to distinguish from reality.

Technical Specification

HAPKE BRDF

Our shading model combines HAPKE BRDF1 and Single Scatter Albedo, providing scientifically accurate results. Texture each planet with albedo, roughness, height, and emission maps, all supported through a PBR workflow.

- Single Scatter Albedo2
- Textured Planets
- Use PBR workflow

- Albedo
- Roughness
- Height
- Emission

Planets

Planets with real-world measurements and scale them down for easier use. Multiple rotation and positioning options. Each sphere interacts with lighting, casting realistic shadows and indirect light effects on other planets.

- Input Real World Scale & Scale down
- Move and Rotate different methods
- Spheres, multiple planets cast shadows & indirect light

Phases

Each layer in your planet’s atmosphere can be fine-tuned for realistic light behavior, using the Draine Phase model with Cornette-Shanks, Rayleigh for molecular scattering, and Mie for aerosol scattering.

- For individual layers
- Draine Phase model3

- Includes Cornette-Shanks4
- Molecular Phase (Rayleigh)
- Aerosol Phase (Mie)

6 Layer atmosphere

Rich atmospheres with six interactive layers that scatter, absorb, and emit light naturally. Each layer casts shadows, with texture-driven density and height profiles for added realism, including options for stunning procedural auroras.

- Layers Sum and Absorb
- Single Scattering Model
- Scatter, Absorb & Emit
- Cast shadows
- Texture-driven Density & Height Profile
- Procedural Aurora texture

Spectral rendering

Precise light wavelengths to achieve accurate color representation in your scenes.

- Input coefficients for 470, 650, 700 𝑛m wavelengths

Rings

Stunning, customizable rings that cast realistic shadows onto the planet and atmosphere. This creates a rich interplay of light and shadow, perfect for cinematic planetary visuals.

- Shadows
- Indirect Light

Background Stars

A procedurally generated star background to offer an out of the box a universe-like look.
